# Tree Base Experimental

This project has utilised 3 tree based model models from simple to more complex.

## Models used 

- Decision Tree
- Random Forest
- Extra Tree
- SVC

## Purpose

Identifying potentials customers for loaning and minimising the financial loss by predicting customer'credit rating (poor/standard/good).

## Explanation
In this experimental, I utilise the tree based models to categorise the group of the customers, perventing the bank from offer the loan to customer who cannot afford to payback.
From my perspective, this project encourage my curiosity in customer behaviour and the possibility (insight), that the model will provide to me.

Moreover, this experiment improve my capability on my ML skills, making me realised; the good model will not be made without good preparations: data cleaning, espescially BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING (5W).
